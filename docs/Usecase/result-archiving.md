## Result Archiving Module

The Result Archiving module ensures long-term preservation of electoral data while strictly protecting voter privacy through automated anonymization and masterlist purging. It permanently archives cryptographic ballots, locks finalized tallies into read-only historical records, and provides transparent public access to past election outcomes without compromising individual voter confidentiality.

- [Event Archiving](/docs/Usecase/Result%20Archiving/event-data-archiving.md)
- [Historical Results Retrieval](/docs/Usecase/Result%20Archiving/historical-results-retrieval.md)

---

### Key Features

- **Privacy Safeguards**: Irreversible scrubbing of session metadata and voter identifiers
- **Cold Storage**: Secure transfer of anonymized ballots to read-only archive tables
- **Masterlist Purging**: Automated deletion of sensitive student data post-conclusion
- **Historical Transparency**: Public access to past election outcomes and statistics
- **Audit Compliance**: Preserved mathematical integrity for future verification
